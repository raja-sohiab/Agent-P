# Email 1 — Business Type Intelligence

## 1. Purpose

This document helps Agent-P understand different types of businesses when generating Email 1.

It provides general business reasoning, including:

- What the business typically provides
- Who it may serve
- What customers may need
- What situations may create demand for the service

This document is NOT an ICP qualification system.

All incoming leads are assumed to be qualified upstream.

---

# 2. Core Rule

Never reject a lead because its business type is not listed here.

The categories below are reasoning references.

The agent must be able to handle businesses outside these categories.

The objective is:

Understand the actual business
→ identify its actual service
→ identify its likely audience
→ identify a plausible customer situation
→ write relevant messaging

# 19. Hybrid Businesses

Some businesses serve both businesses and consumers.

Do not assume one audience without evidence.

Determine:

- Which audience is relevant to the company's service
- Which audience creates the clearest commercial opportunity
- Which audience can logically connect to outbound prospecting

If both audiences are relevant, choose the strongest angle for the specific email.

# 21. Unfamiliar Businesses

If the business does not fit any category:

Do not reject it.

Use this process:

1. Determine what the business actually provides.
2. Determine who pays for or uses the service/product.
3. Determine why that customer would need it.
4. Determine the most plausible commercial trigger.
5. Identify the company's relevant service.
6. Apply the PersistIQ value.
7. Write Email 1 using the standard structure.

The business-type library is expandable.

---

# 22. Website Overrides Generic Classification

If a company's website provides specific information that differs from a generic business category, use the website information.

Example:

Generic classification:
Software company

Website:
AI solutions specifically for food manufacturers

Use:

Food manufacturers
→ operational/AI needs
→ AI solution
→ relevant businesses
→ PersistIQ

Do not write a generic software-company email simply because the business falls into the technology category.

---

# 23. Do Not Overgeneralize

Avoid reducing every business to:

- "Businesses that need more customers"
- "Companies looking to grow"
- "Businesses needing help"

These statements are too generic.

Identify the actual service and the customer situation connected to it.

---

# 24. Business Type Is Not ICP

Business type intelligence answers:

"What does this business do and how does its market generally work?"

ICP answers:

"Should this lead qualify for this campaign?"

Agent-P handles the first question.

Agent-P does NOT handle the second.

---

# 25. Primary Principle

Business categories provide context.

They do not replace actual business understanding.

Always prefer:

Actual business information
→ specific service
→ relevant audience
→ plausible situation

over:

Generic category
→ generic assumption
→ generic email.
