# Email 1 — Reference Examples

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how the PersistIQ Email 1 reference examples should be used.

The reference dataset contains 501 examples pairing:

- Business Type
- First Email Message

These examples are reference material for learning the desired Email 1 reasoning, structure, tone, specificity, and level of concision.

They are not templates.

---

## 2. Source Dataset

The complete reference dataset is stored separately as:

`501-REFERENCE-EXAMPLES.csv`

The CSV is the authoritative source for the examples.

Do not modify the original examples to create new messaging rules.

---

## 3. How to Use the Examples

Agent-P should use the examples to understand patterns such as:

- How different business types are interpreted
- How audiences are identified
- How customer situations are framed
- How services are connected to customer needs
- How PersistIQ value is introduced
- How soft CTAs are used
- How concise the final email should be
- How natural business-specific relevance is established

The agent should learn the reasoning behind the examples.

---

## 4. Examples Are Not Templates

Never treat an example as a template.

Do not:

- Copy an example
- Replace the business name
- Replace the service
- Replace the industry
- Perform noun substitution
- Reproduce the same sentence skeleton unnecessarily

Instead:

Reference example
→ understand why it works
→ identify the underlying reasoning
→ apply the reasoning to the new business
→ write original language

---

## 5. Business Type Matching

When a new lead matches or resembles a business type in the reference dataset, the agent may use the relevant examples to understand how that type of business can be approached.

However, matching a business type does not mean copying its email.

The new email must still be based on the actual lead.

---

## 6. Unseen Business Types

If a business type is not represented in the reference dataset:

Do not reject the lead.

Use the general Email 1 reasoning framework:

Business
→ Audience
→ Situation / Need
→ Service
→ PersistIQ Value
→ Soft CTA

The reference examples may still be used to identify the closest commercial reasoning pattern.

---

## 7. Website Information Has Priority

When website information is available, use it to understand the actual business.

A reference example should never override accurate information about the current lead.

Example:

Reference example:
Generic software company

Current lead website:
AI solutions specifically for food manufacturers

The current lead should be treated according to its actual business and customer context.

---

## 8. Example Selection

Examples should be selected based on underlying commercial similarity rather than superficial word similarity.

Relevant similarity may include:

- Same type of customer
- Similar customer need
- Similar commercial trigger
- Similar service
- Similar buying situation
- Similar business model

Do not select an example simply because it contains similar words.

---

## 9. Learning From Examples

The examples collectively demonstrate the desired writing behavior.

Agent-P should learn:

### Relevance

The email starts from a plausible business/customer situation.

### Specificity

The situation is relevant enough to demonstrate business understanding.

### Concision

The message communicates the idea without unnecessary explanation.

### Product Connection

PersistIQ is connected naturally to the relevant audience.

### CTA

The message ends with a soft invitation.

### Tone

The writing remains human, direct, professional, and low pressure.

---

## 10. Example-Based Reasoning

The agent may internally use examples as references when deciding:

- Which audience is logical
- Which commercial situation is plausible
- Which service connection makes sense
- How specific the language should be
- How to phrase the PersistIQ value

The final email must still be independently generated.

---

## 11. Avoid Overfitting

Do not assume that one example defines how an entire business category should always be written.

The 501 examples represent multiple valid ways to construct Email 1.

Use them collectively.

The agent should generalize.

---

## 12. No ICP Learning

The examples must not be used to create an ICP filter.

Agent-P must not infer:

"This business type was not represented in the examples, therefore it should be rejected."

The examples are for email customization only.

Lead qualification occurs upstream.

---

## 13. Product Consistency

All examples in this reference set are PersistIQ Email 1 examples.

Do not use them to import:

- Other product value propositions
- Other product messaging
- Other campaign rules

Only the underlying writing and reasoning principles may be generalized.

---

## 14. Example Quality Standard

When reviewing an example, focus on whether it demonstrates:

- Correct business understanding
- Clear audience relevance
- Plausible customer situation
- Logical service connection
- Natural PersistIQ value
- Soft CTA
- Concise writing
- Human tone

These are the characteristics Agent-P should reproduce.

---

## 15. Reference Hierarchy

When generating Email 1, use knowledge in this priority:

1. Actual current lead information
2. Available website information
3. Agent-P rules
4. PersistIQ product knowledge
5. Email 1 structure and reasoning patterns
6. Relevant reference examples
7. General business-category knowledge

Reference examples should support reasoning, not override current lead facts.

---

## 16. Final Principle

The goal is not to make the new email look like one of the 501 examples.

The goal is to make the new email demonstrate the same quality of reasoning and writing that the 501 examples demonstrate.
