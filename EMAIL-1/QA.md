# Email 1 QA

For every row, verify:

- Email 1 contains 15–25 words. This is a hard production requirement. Count words during final QA; rewrite any message outside the range before release.
- Business, service, customer type, need, direct outreach offer, commercial benefit, and soft CTA are coherent.
- Customer type is evidence-supported and not forced or invented.
- The message is independently reasoned and not copied from a reference or another row.
- No fabrication, fake personalization, “I noticed,” “I came across,” guarantees, hype, unnecessary links, aggressive CTA, or em dash character `—`.
- No generic fallback wording.

After all rows are written, perform batch-level construction review. Exact duplicates are not enough: flag repeated sentence skeletons, openings, proposition framing, or CTAs whose nouns or customer types merely change. Revise affected rows naturally without using a phrase list or fixed rotation.
