# Email 1 — Structure

## 1. Purpose

Email 1 establishes initial relevance and introduces the PersistIQ value proposition.

The email should make the prospect understand:

- Why the topic is relevant to their business
- What type of customer situation is being addressed
- How their service fits that situation
- How PersistIQ could help create relevant conversations

The preferred output is concise and normally consists of two sentences.

---

# 2. Core Structure

The underlying structure is:

Business
→ Audience
→ Situation / Need
→ Service
→ PersistIQ Value
→ Soft CTA

This is the reasoning structure.

It is not a rigid writing template.

---

# 3. Sentence 1

Sentence 1 establishes the business-specific relevance.

It should normally combine some or all of:

- Target audience
- Customer situation
- Customer need
- Relevant commercial trigger
- Company's service

The sentence should describe a situation in which the company's service would logically matter.

Conceptually:

"[Relevant audience] often [faces / reaches / considers] [situation or need], where [company service] becomes relevant."

Do not use this exact wording mechanically.

The final sentence must sound natural for the specific business.

---

# 4. Sentence 2

Sentence 2 connects the established situation to PersistIQ.

It should communicate that PersistIQ could help the company:

- Create relevant conversations
- Reach relevant prospects
- Reach people considering the service
- Reach businesses considering the service
- Surface relevant opportunities
- Connect with potential customers

The sentence should finish with a soft CTA.

Conceptually:

"PersistIQ could help [business] create/reach/surface [relevant opportunity], [soft CTA]?"

Again, this is a reasoning guide rather than a fixed template.

---

# 5. Two-Sentence Flow

The preferred flow is:

Sentence 1:
Business-specific relevance

+

Sentence 2:
PersistIQ value + soft CTA

Example reasoning:

A roofing company
→ serves property owners
→ property owners may face aging or damaged roofs
→ roofing services address that need
→ PersistIQ could help reach people considering roofing work
→ soft CTA

The final email should express that reasoning naturally and concisely.

---

# 6. Relevance Before Product

Do not lead with PersistIQ.

Weak:

"PersistIQ helps companies generate more leads."

Better:

"Property owners dealing with aging or damaged roofs often need help deciding between repairs and replacement."

Then connect PersistIQ:

"PersistIQ could help create more conversations with people considering that work. Worth exploring?"

The business relevance should establish the reason for the product value.

---

# 7. Audience Selection

Choose the audience most logically connected to the company's service.

Examples:

### Home Services

Business:
Roofing company

Audience:
Property owners

Potential situation:
Repairs, replacement, maintenance, storm damage

Service:
Roofing

---

### Professional Services

Business:
Accounting firm

Audience:
Business owners

Potential situation:
Growing business, changing financial needs, accounting requirements

Service:
Accounting

---

### Technology

Business:
Software consultancy

Audience:
Businesses or teams

Potential situation:
Evaluating software, automation, technology improvements

Service:
Software development / technology consulting

---

### Financial Services

Business:
Wealth-management firm

Audience:
Individuals, families, or business owners

Potential situation:
Financial planning, investment decisions, long-term planning

Service:
Wealth management / financial planning

---

### Real Estate

Business:
Real estate company

Audience:
Buyers, sellers, investors, landlords, or property owners

Potential situation:
Buying, selling, investing, managing property

Service:
Real estate services

These are examples of reasoning categories, not fixed audience assignments.

---

# 8. Situation Selection

Choose the situation that creates the strongest logical connection between the audience and the service.

Possible situations include:

- Planning
- Evaluation
- Purchase
- Replacement
- Maintenance
- Repair
- Upgrade
- Expansion
- Growth
- New project
- Problem resolution
- Service requirement
- Change in business needs

Do not force a situation simply because it appears frequently in the reference examples.

---

# 9. Service Connection

The situation must logically lead to the company's service.

Example:

Property owner
→ aging roof
→ needs inspection/repair/replacement
→ roofing company

Not:

Property owner
→ growing business
→ roofing company

The reasoning must make commercial sense.

---

# 10. PersistIQ Value Connection

Once the business situation is established, determine what type of prospect the company would want to reach.

Then connect PersistIQ to that prospect.

Conceptually:

Business
→ Customer
→ Need
→ Service
→ Relevant prospect
→ PersistIQ

Example:

Dental practice
→ patients
→ dental treatment need
→ dental services
→ people considering treatment
→ PersistIQ could help create conversations with relevant prospects

---

# 11. CTA

The CTA should be soft and natural.

Suitable CTA concepts include:

- Worth exploring?
- Open to seeing how?
- Worth a look?
- Open to exploring it?
- Would it make sense to explore?
- Make sense to discuss?

Use natural variation.

Do not make the CTA aggressive.

Avoid:

- Book a call
- Schedule a demo
- Let's jump on a call
- When are you free?
- Book 15 minutes
- Grab time on my calendar

unless a future messaging specification explicitly requires them.

---

# 12. Sentence Length

Keep both sentences concise.

Avoid:

- Long explanations
- Multiple clauses
- Product descriptions
- Lists
- Background information
- Excessive context

The reader should understand the proposition quickly.

---

# 13. Natural Variation

The two-sentence structure should remain consistent while the wording varies naturally.

Variation can come from:

- Different audience descriptions
- Different situation framing
- Different sentence openings
- Different verbs
- Different transitions
- Different PersistIQ value wording
- Different CTA wording

The structure should remain recognizable without becoming repetitive.

---

# 14. Do Not Use a Mechanical Template

Do not generate every email using:

"Businesses that [X] often need [Y]. PersistIQ could help you [Z]. Worth exploring?"

That structure may be useful as an internal reasoning reference, but using it repeatedly would create obvious template repetition.

The agent should understand the pattern rather than reproduce the formula.

---

# 15. Business-Type Adaptation

The structure must work across different business types.

The agent should adapt:

- Audience
- Situation
- Service
- Commercial trigger
- Vocabulary
- Level of specificity

The structure remains stable.

The reasoning changes.

---

# 16. Unfamiliar Businesses

If the business type does not appear in the reference examples:

1. Understand the business.
2. Identify its service.
3. Identify its likely audience.
4. Identify a plausible customer situation.
5. Identify the closest reasoning pattern.
6. Apply the same structural logic.
7. Connect PersistIQ's value.
8. Add a soft CTA.

Do not force the business into an unrelated example.

---

# 17. Website-Based Structure

When website information is available, use it to improve the structure.

Example reasoning:

Website:
Provides AI solutions for food manufacturers

↓

Audience:
Food manufacturing businesses

↓

Situation:
Evaluating AI / automation for operational challenges

↓

Service:
AI/software consulting

↓

PersistIQ:
Reach businesses considering those solutions

↓

Email:

Sentence 1:
Relevant food-manufacturing situation

Sentence 2:
PersistIQ value + soft CTA

Website information should improve specificity without creating unsupported claims.

---

# 18. Final Structure Test

Before approving Email 1, verify:

### Sentence 1

Does it establish a clear and plausible business-specific situation?

### Sentence 2

Does it connect that situation to PersistIQ's value?

### CTA

Is the CTA soft?

### Overall

Does the email read naturally as one thought?

If the email feels like:

"business observation + unrelated sales pitch"

rewrite it.

The connection between the two sentences must be logical.

---

# 19. Target Outcome

The final Email 1 should feel like:

"I understand what this business does, I understand who may need it, I understand a situation where their service matters, and I can see why PersistIQ might help them reach those people."

It should not feel like:

"I found a business category and inserted it into a sales template."

---

# 20. Core Formula

The underlying formula is:

RELEVANCE
+
SERVICE CONTEXT
+
PERSISTIQ VALUE
+
SOFT CTA

The formula should remain consistent.

The language should remain natural.
