# Email 1 Reasoning

Email 1 must be independently reasoned from the lead's evidence. Use this sequence as reasoning, not a sentence template:

Business/service → evidence-supported customer type → customer need or situation → relevant service → direct outreach offer → commercial benefit → soft CTA.

Use the actual customer type when supported. Do not use “prospect” as a generic substitute, and do not invent or force a customer type. Prefer a broader accurate angle over unsupported specificity. Direct offer-led language is required; the offer must be connected to the business and customer situation, not appended as a generic “we could help.” PersistIQ naming is optional.

Offer construction must vary at the reasoning and sentence-construction level across leads. Do not mechanically rotate phrases, copy examples, or reuse a fixed formula. Naturalness and relevance take priority over artificial uniqueness.

The 501 reference examples demonstrate quality and reasoning only. They are not templates or a phrase library.
