# Email 2 Rules

Email 2 must read the exact Email 1 generated for its row. Continue the same core proposition, remain concise and low pressure, and do not introduce a new commercial angle or simply paraphrase Email 1.

Vary naturally across rows in opening, sentence structure, proposition framing, and CTA. Changing a business name, customer type, or a few nouns inside one formula is not meaningful variation. Do not repeatedly use constructions such as “Following up,” “Checking in,” or “Would reaching [audience] support [business]?” Do not use mechanical synonym replacement, a phrase library, or a fixed rotation. Do not force variation when relevance or naturalness would suffer.

Use the actual customer type or context from Email 1 when appropriate. Stay connected to the original offer. Use a soft natural CTA. Never use the em dash character `—`.
