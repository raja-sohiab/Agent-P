# Email 3 Rules

Email 3 must read the exact Email 1 and Email 2 for its row. It must introduce one genuinely new, commercially relevant angle while remaining connected to the same business and service.

The new angle may be a different customer situation, buying trigger, decision point, service application, commercial opportunity, or service need. Do not recap Email 1 or Email 2. Do not create another audience merely to satisfy the requirement. An audience-based angle is valid only when it is independently supported by the available business/service evidence and is genuinely the strongest new commercial angle. Even then, never use the formulaic “another relevant audience” construction.

Do not use unsupported specificity; “may” or “could” does not make weak speculation acceptable. Do not introduce a different product, unrelated service, or unrelated campaign objective. Use a soft CTA, remain low pressure, and never use the em dash character `—`.
