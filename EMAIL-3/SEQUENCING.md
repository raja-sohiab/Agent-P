# Email 3 — Sequencing

## 1. Purpose

Email 3 is generated using the actual content and reasoning from:

- Email 1
- Email 2

It must understand what has already been communicated before introducing a new angle.

---

## 2. Required Context

Before writing Email 3, the agent must have access to:

1. Business information
2. Company/service understanding
3. Email 1
4. Email 2
5. The commercial angle used in Email 1
6. The purpose of Email 2
7. The PersistIQ value proposition

---

## 3. Sequence Logic

The sequence must progress as:

**Email 1**
→ Initial relevance + value

**Email 2**
→ Follow-up based on Email 1

**Email 3**
→ New angle / additional reason to engage

---

## 4. Email 1 Memory

Email 3 must identify the primary commercial idea already used in Email 1.

Record internally:

- Main audience
- Customer situation
- Commercial trigger
- Service need
- PersistIQ connection

This prevents Email 3 from accidentally repeating Email 1.

---

## 5. Email 2 Memory

Email 3 must also understand what Email 2 communicated.

Identify:

- What Email 2 followed up on
- Whether it reinforced Email 1
- What wording or value was already emphasized
- Whether Email 2 introduced any additional context

Email 3 must not simply repeat the same follow-up.

---

## 6. Angle Separation

Before writing Email 3, compare the proposed angle against both previous emails.

Ask:

> "Is this actually a different commercial reason?"

If the answer is no, reject the angle.

---

## 7. Valid Progression

A valid sequence can look like:

**Email 1**
→ One customer situation

**Email 2**
→ Follow-up on that situation

**Email 3**
→ Another customer situation

Or:

**Email 1**
→ One audience

**Email 2**
→ Follow-up

**Email 3**
→ A genuinely new commercially relevant angle, such as a different customer situation, buying trigger, decision point, service application, commercial opportunity, or service need. An audience change is only valid when it is the strongest independently supported angle, and the formulaic "another relevant audience" construction is not used.

Or:

**Email 1**
→ One commercial trigger

**Email 2**
→ Follow-up

**Email 3**
→ Another relevant commercial trigger

The specific progression depends on the business.

---

## 8. Same Business Boundary

The sequence must remain anchored to the same company.

Email 3 cannot use a new angle that changes:

- The company's service
- The company's market
- The business model
- The product being promoted

unless the available evidence clearly supports that relationship.

---

## 9. Same PersistIQ Value

The PersistIQ value proposition remains consistent across the sequence.

Email 3 may express the value differently, but it should still communicate the underlying benefit:

**Helping the business create relevant outbound conversations and opportunities.**

---

## 10. No Sequence Reset

Do not treat Email 3 as a completely new first email.

Avoid:

- Reintroducing the company
- Repeating the original pitch
- Starting from scratch
- Repeating the same opening logic

Email 3 belongs to an existing conversation.

---

## 11. No Artificial Continuation

Do not force continuity through phrases such as:

- Just following up again
- Circling back
- As mentioned
- Following up on my last email
- One more thought

unless such wording is genuinely necessary.

The new commercial angle itself should create the progression.

---

## 12. New Angle Selection

After understanding Email 1 and Email 2, generate potential additional angles from:

- Business services
- Customer types
- Customer situations
- Buying triggers
- Planning events
- Replacement needs
- Evaluation needs
- Maintenance needs
- Growth-related needs
- Other legitimate service-related opportunities

Select the strongest relevant angle.

---

## 13. Angle Priority

Prefer an angle in this order:

1. Directly supported by company information
2. Strongly connected to the company's service
3. Supported by common commercial behavior
4. Reasonable contextual inference

Do not prioritize creativity over evidence.

---

## 14. Avoiding Repetition

The agent should compare the proposed Email 3 angle with:

### Email 1

What was the original commercial reason?

### Email 2

What was the follow-up reason?

### Proposed Email 3

What is different?

If the answer is difficult to explain in one sentence, the angle is probably too similar.

---

## 15. Wording Repetition

Do not unnecessarily reuse the exact:

- Opening phrase
- Audience description
- Trigger description
- Service description
- Value phrase
- CTA

Some natural repetition is acceptable.

Mechanical repetition is not.

---

## 16. Commercial Continuity

Although the angle changes, the underlying commercial conversation remains consistent.

The sequence should feel like:

**Reason 1**
→ **follow-up**
→ **Reason 2**

Not:

**Topic 1**
→ **Topic 1**
→ **Random Topic 2**

---

## 17. Email 3 Should Add Information

A useful Email 3 should give the recipient something new to consider.

The new information can be:

- Another customer situation
- Another relevant prospect group
- Another service need
- Another buying trigger
- Another commercial opportunity

---

## 18. If No Strong New Angle Exists

Do not invent one.

Instead, use the closest legitimate extension of the original commercial opportunity while making the distinction clear.

Do not fabricate company-specific circumstances to satisfy the "new angle" requirement.

---

## 19. CTA Continuity

The CTA should remain low pressure.

Do not escalate from:

Email 1 → soft CTA

Email 2 → soft CTA

Email 3 → aggressive meeting request

The sequence should maintain a consistent conversational tone.

---

## 20. Sequence Integrity Check

Before output, verify:

### Email 1
What was the original angle?

### Email 2
What did Email 2 follow up on?

### Email 3
What is the new angle?

### Business
Are all three emails about the same business?

### Service
Are all three connected to the actual service?

### Product
Are all three using PersistIQ's value proposition?

### Repetition
Does Email 3 avoid repeating the previous commercial idea?

### Evidence
Is the new angle supported?

### Tone
Does the sequence remain natural and low pressure?

---

## 21. Final Sequencing Principle

Email 3 should feel like the natural next step in the conversation:

**Email 1:** "Here is one relevant opportunity."

**Email 2:** "Following up on that opportunity."

**Email 3:** "Here is another relevant opportunity."

The sequence progresses through **relevance**, not pressure.
