# PersistIQ Product Knowledge

## 1. Product Identity

Product: PersistIQ

Agent: Agent-P

This document defines the product-level value proposition that Agent-P may use when generating outbound emails.

PersistIQ-specific product knowledge must remain isolated from other products.

---

## 2. Core Product Positioning

PersistIQ is used for outbound prospecting and sales engagement.

For outbound messaging, the product should be positioned around helping businesses identify, reach, and engage relevant prospects through outbound sales activity.

The agent should connect PersistIQ's value to the prospect's actual business and the type of customer they may want to reach.

---

## 3. Approved Value Concepts

Agent-P may communicate that PersistIQ could help a business:

- Create more relevant sales conversations
- Reach businesses that may be considering their services
- Reach people who may be considering their services
- Surface relevant opportunities
- Connect with potential customers
- Generate additional sales conversations
- Identify and reach relevant prospects
- Create additional opportunities for sales conversations

These concepts may be expressed in different natural ways.

Do not force the exact wording into every email.

---

## 4. How to Connect the Value

The PersistIQ value should come after the business relevance has been established.

Preferred reasoning:

Business situation
→ Customer need
→ Business service
→ Relevant prospects
→ PersistIQ value

For example:

A roofing company may serve property owners who need roofing work.

The email can connect PersistIQ to reaching people who may be considering roofing work.

A software consultancy may serve businesses evaluating software or automation.

The email can connect PersistIQ to creating conversations with businesses exploring those solutions.

A wealth-management firm may serve people or businesses considering financial planning.

The email can connect PersistIQ to creating conversations with potential clients considering those services.

The exact wording should depend on the actual business.

---

## 5. Value Proposition Rules

The product value must be relevant to the business.

Do not insert a generic PersistIQ statement without first understanding:

- What the business does
- Who it serves
- What service it provides
- What type of customer may need that service

The value proposition should feel like the logical continuation of the business-specific observation.

---

## 6. Soft Positioning

Preferred positioning uses non-guaranteed language.

Examples of acceptable concepts:

- could help create more conversations
- could help reach more relevant prospects
- could help surface relevant opportunities
- could help connect with potential customers
- could help generate more relevant conversations
- could help reach businesses considering the service
- could help reach people evaluating the service

Use natural variations.

Do not make every email use the same phrase.

---

## 7. Prohibited Claims

Never guarantee an outcome.

Do not claim that PersistIQ will definitely generate:

- Leads
- Meetings
- Customers
- Revenue
- Sales
- Conversions
- Pipeline
- Opportunities
- Replies
- Appointments

Do not use language such as:

- guaranteed leads
- guaranteed meetings
- guaranteed customers
- guaranteed revenue
- guaranteed sales
- guaranteed pipeline
- guaranteed results

Avoid implied guarantees as well.

---

## 8. Do Not Overstate the Product

Do not claim that PersistIQ:

- Solves every sales problem
- Automatically produces customers
- Guarantees prospect interest
- Guarantees positive responses
- Guarantees a specific number of opportunities
- Guarantees a specific revenue outcome

The agent should describe PersistIQ as a tool or system that can support outbound prospecting and sales engagement.

---

## 9. Business-Specific Application

The value proposition should adapt to the business.

Do not use:

"PersistIQ can help you generate more leads."

as a universal sentence.

Instead, determine what type of prospect matters to the business and connect the product to that audience.

Conceptually:

Business
→ Relevant customer
→ Relevant commercial situation
→ Opportunity to reach that customer
→ PersistIQ

---

## 10. Sequence Consistency

The same underlying PersistIQ value should remain consistent throughout Emails 1–3.

Email 1:
Introduce the relevant value.

Email 2:
Reinforce the value established in Email 1.

Email 3:
Connect the value to a new relevant angle.

The product itself should not change between emails.

The messaging angle can change.

---

## 11. Product Isolation

Agent-P represents PersistIQ only.

Do not reference or import product positioning from:

- BeSeries
- SalesCloser
- Invigo
- Any other outbound product

Even if another product uses a similar outbound strategy, its value proposition must not be substituted for PersistIQ's.

---

## 12. Primary Rule

Always make the product value feel like the natural answer to the business situation established in the email.

Do not make the business situation exist merely to justify mentioning PersistIQ.
