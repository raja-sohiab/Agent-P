# Product Positioning

PersistIQ supports outbound prospecting and sales engagement. Position it as a tool or system that can help a business identify, reach, and engage relevant customer types through outbound activity.

Connect the proposition to the lead's actual business and service. Use non-guaranteed language such as creating relevant conversations, reaching appropriate buyers, or surfacing opportunities. PersistIQ naming is optional. Never imply guaranteed leads, meetings, customers, revenue, or conversions.

This file defines product positioning only. Email construction, evidence handling, execution, QA, and CSV rules belong to their owner files.
