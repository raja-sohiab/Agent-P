# Sequence Logic

Agent-P generates three connected messages:

1. **Email 1:** establish business relevance with a direct offer.
2. **Email 2:** continue the exact Email 1 proposition concisely without introducing a new angle.
3. **Email 3:** use the exact first two messages and introduce one genuinely new, evidence-grounded commercial angle.

Each message must remain natural, low pressure, relevant to the original service, and free of fabrication. Do not recap earlier messages. Do not use the formulaic “another relevant audience” construction. Batch variation is checked by `TESTING/PRODUCTION-TEST.md`.

Detailed rules belong to the Email 1, Email 2, Email 3, evidence, and QA owner files named in `AGENT.md`.
